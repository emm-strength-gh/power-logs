RPE Estimator

Turn a one-rep max into a full RPE → %1RM load chart, with a separate curve per lift. Runs as its own self-contained tool — it keeps its own settings and doesn't touch your loaded programs.

Can't see the tool? It lives in rpe-estimator.html next to this file — make sure both are uploaded together. Open it in a new tab.

Velocity Tracker

Measure barbell velocity from a video clip and get mean concentric velocity, estimated RPE, ROM and velocity loss per rep. Set a personal velocity anchor per lift (the ⚙ on the results step) for the RPE estimates to tighten up. Runs as its own self-contained tool and doesn't touch your loaded programs.

Can't see the tracker? It lives in VBT.html next to this file — make sure both are uploaded together. Open it in a new tab.

Compare programs

About

Power Logs

A training log built for powerlifting. Each lifter’s program becomes a week-by-week checklist: tick sets done or skipped, add notes, and follow tonnage, number of lifts and heaviest top sets in Analytics.

Alongside the log: an RPE estimator, a Velocity Tracker that reads bar speed from a video, a plate calculator and a rest timer. Coaches write and adjust programs in Manage Program and the Program Hub, and with an account everything syncs between coach and lifter.

EmmStrength coaching

EmmStrength is powerlifting coaching: individual programming for the squat, bench press and deadlift, from building general strength to peaking for a meet, for classic and equipped lifters.

Your program here is written by your coach and adjusted from what you log, so tick your sets and leave notes on how they felt.
