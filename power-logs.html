Manage program

Preview, edit, and reorganise the loaded program — edit, add, delete, or reorder exercises, add or delete days, delete weeks, and replace or merge a day or a whole week from another program you import. The imported program is temporary and is discarded when you go back.

Progression and load-management views for the whole loaded program. Estimated 1RM and volume are built from the programmed sets; adherence and ACWR use what you've actually ticked off as Done and Skipped.

An elite-style meet-day ramp from an empty 20 kg bar up through your opener, second, and third attempt (your 1RM). Bench gets a couple of extra warm-up steps since it's less fatiguing; deadlift gets fewer since it's usually the last, most taxing lift of the day.

The program builders — pick a methodology, enter the lifter's competition maxes, and generate a dashboard-ready CSV. RPE Estimator

Turn a one-rep max into a full RPE → %1RM load chart, with a separate curve per lift. Runs as its own self-contained tool — it keeps its own settings and doesn't touch your loaded programs.

Can't see the tool? It lives in rpe-estimator.html next to this file — make sure both are uploaded together. Open it in a new tab.

Velocity Tracker

Measure barbell velocity from a video clip and get mean concentric velocity, estimated RPE, ROM and velocity loss per rep. Set a personal velocity anchor per lift (the ⚙ on the results step) for the RPE estimates to tighten up. Runs as its own self-contained tool and doesn't touch your loaded programs.

Can't see the tracker? It lives in VBT.html next to this file — make sure both are uploaded together. Open it in a new tab.

Compare programs

Load two programs side by side — a CSV training log or a Spotter JSON export from either one — to compare tonnage, number of lifts, intensity, top sets, and estimated 1RM week by week. Nothing here is saved or added to your lifter list.
